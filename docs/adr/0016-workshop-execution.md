# Workshop execution: stages, extra work and the live floor

A confirmed visit now has a life on the floor. Staff record seven stages in strict order, each once: CHECKED_IN, INSPECTING, SERVICING, QUALITY_CHECK, WASHING, READY, HANDED_OVER. The 60-minute target runs from check-in until the car is ready for collection; handover is recorded separately when the customer collects, so a slow collection never counts against the hour. The customer app reads the same record as a progress view with elapsed minutes against the target, the stage timeline, a coffee voucher issued at check-in, and any extra work waiting for an answer. The operations console reads it as a floor board polled every five seconds.

Extra work is a scope change: itemised amounts in cents, a note, a total, proposed by the hub and approved or declined by the customer. Approval means settle at handover; no payment is taken in the app for it. A proposal can be raised on a confirmed visit before or during the visit but not once the car is ready. A visit with a proposal waiting is not blocked by a flag: it simply does not advance until the customer answers, and the board says so. Each decision and each stage event goes to the outbox in the same transaction.

The record is a new `workshop` module with two additive tables. `visit_stage_event` holds one row per stage per visit with who recorded it and when, under a unique constraint, so a retry conflicts rather than duplicates. `scope_change` holds the items as JSON with the derived total. Writes take the visit row lock first, so two staff tablets cannot record stages out of order. The module reads `visit_hold` and `resource_allocation` by SQL and the hub's timezone through the hubs API; it depends on nothing internal to booking. Operators reach it under `/v1/hubs/{hubId}/floor` and `/visits/{id}/...`; customers under `/v1/customer/appointments/{id}/progress` and `/scope-changes/{scopeId}/decision`, with the same ownership and hub-membership checks as the rest of the platform.

Because a car on the floor has passed its start time, the customer's bookings list now keeps a confirmed visit for four hours after its start. An unpaid hold past its start still disappears.

A demo-only floor simulation, off by default and logged loudly when on, checks in confirmed visits whose start has just passed and advances cars through the stages on a configurable pace. It never moves a car past READY and never moves one with a proposal waiting. It exists so one person can demonstrate the app and the board together; the production configuration must leave `serv60.demo.floor-simulation.enabled` unset.

Rollout is additive (V28). Roll the backend out before the console and app builds that use the floor, and roll back by reverting the application without dropping the tables. Push notifications, stage photos, the walkaround signature and the staff tablet view remain to be built on top of this record.
