# Serv60 visual assets

The images and the film in `dist/assets` are generated concept assets. They illustrate the intended destination and are included as editable website resources, rather than images of an existing location. No source-generation utility is required to deploy the website.

## October 2026: the renders and the film

Six renders of the hub, all 1168×784 and saved at 1168 and 800 px wide, replaced the earlier stills: the coffee lounge at dusk (`coffee.webp`), the barber chair before its arched mirror (`barber.webp`), the private workspace through its glass door (`work.webp`), the workshop with a car on the lift (`workshop.webp`), the reception desk (`reception.webp`) and the exterior at blue hour (`hub-exterior.webp`). The exterior and the workshop also exist blurred and darkened in `scene/` as the journey's backdrops, and the exterior is the 1200×630 Open Graph card `og-hub.jpg`. The hero film is a 30-second generated sequence of the hub, encoded for the web in `video/`; its opening frame is `hub-film-poster.webp`, three of its frames sit in `film/`, and its closing aerial is `finale-still.webp`.

## Original image directions

- **Car:** A full unbranded champagne-silver sedan in a front three-quarter side view, nose right, black panoramic roof, polished alloy wheels and clean studio highlights. Transparent background, no floor, no baked-in shadow and no manufacturer badge. The optimized asset is 1774×887.
- **Coffee:** A ceramic flat white on a walnut table in a sunlit café, with ivory lounge seating, an olive tree and Cape mountain outlook. Warm architectural photography.
- **Barber:** A saddle-brown leather barber chair, arched mirror, warm oak and plaster in a softly lit modern studio. Portrait composition.
- **Work:** A private workspace seen through a glass doorway, with walnut desk, monitor, cream chair and acoustic surfaces. Warm neutral light and portrait composition.

## Hub architectural concept

**Mode:** Generate a new image using the built-in image-generation tool. No reference image; opaque background.


**Website asset (retired October 2026):** `dist/assets/hub-architecture.webp`, 1916×821 pixels, was used in the opening and hub sections until the film and the exterior render replaced it. The prompt is kept for the record.

## Exact prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: full-bleed architectural website hero for the Serv60 automotive lifestyle hub concept. Create one spectacular photorealistic premium architectural photograph, ultrawide landscape composition, approximately 21:9. A contemporary automotive service and lifestyle hub at blue-hour dusk in a South African suburban setting. A long low sculptural charcoal-black building with a refined floating canopy, glass walls, warm amber interiors: a beautiful coffee lounge is clearly visible on the left side of the building, immaculate open service bays are visible on the right. One unbranded champagne silver luxury sedan approaches along the clean foreground drive, nose toward the right in a restrained three-quarter side view. Realistic human-scale architecture, carefully composed premium automotive campaign photography, quiet and inviting rather than industrial. Travertine accents, dark brushed metal, soft interior downlights, subtle olive landscaping, delicate long strip lighting under the canopy. Building occupies the right two thirds and lower half; upper left has an uncluttered very dark blue charcoal sky and generous negative space suitable for website headline overlay. Dramatic but credible natural lighting, rich black shadows, warm interior glow, subtle road reflections, no excessive orange neon, no science-fiction, no cartoon, no illustration, no exaggerated car proportions. No typography, no logos, no watermarks, no recognizable manufacturer badges, no signs or written labels, no border. This is a concept architectural visualization, not a photograph of an existing location. The image itself must show only the scene; do not draw a website or interface.
