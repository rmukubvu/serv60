Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: full-bleed architectural website hero for the Serv60 automotive lifestyle hub concept. Create one spectacular photorealistic premium architectural photograph, ultrawide landscape composition, approximately 21:9. A contemporary automotive service and lifestyle hub at blue-hour dusk in a South African suburban setting. One unbranded champagne silver luxury sedan approaches along the clean foreground drive, nose toward the right in a restrained three-quarter side view. Realistic human-scale architecture, carefully composed premium automotive campaign photography, quiet and inviting rather than industrial. Travertine accents, dark brushed metal, soft interior downlights, subtle olive landscaping, delicate long strip lighting under the canopy. Building occupies the right two thirds and lower half; upper left has an uncluttered very dark blue charcoal sky and generous negative space suitable for website headline overlay. Dramatic but credible natural lighting, rich black shadows, warm interior glow, subtle road reflections, no excessive orange neon, no science-fiction, no cartoon, no illustration, no exaggerated car proportions. No typography, no logos, no watermarks, no recognizable manufacturer badges, no signs or written labels, no border. This is a concept architectural visualization, not a photograph of an existing location. The image itself must show only the scene; do not draw a website or interface.
